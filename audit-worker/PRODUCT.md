# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
E-commerce store owners and marketing leads in Egypt and the GCC (many bilingual EN/AR) who requested a free CRO & analytics audit from optimizers.agency. They open the report from an email link, usually on a phone first, then again on a laptop when deciding what to do. Most are not analysts. The job: understand what is broken in their tracking and site, how much it matters, and what to fix first.

## Product Purpose
The audit report (`src/html-report.ts`, one self-contained HTML file per audit) turns 50 checks of a store's GA4, GTM and website into a document an owner can act on. Success is the owner trusting the findings, knowing the top fixes, and booking a call with Optimizers.

## Brand Personality
Expert, calm, precise. Reads like a senior consultant wrote it: quiet confidence, plain language, specific numbers from the store's own data. Never salesy, never alarmist, never cute.

## Anti-references
- AI dark-mode SaaS templates: navy or near-black backgrounds with neon green accents, glowing borders, gradient headings.
- Dashboard scaffolding: hero card with a giant score number, three identical cards in a row, pill badges on everything, filter chips as the main interface.
- Uppercase tracked eyebrows above every section; numbered section markers as decoration.
- Previous versions of this report (navy glow cards, check-square map, tinted pill tags).

Positive reference: Stripe and Linear documentation. Typography-led pages, generous white space, thin dividers instead of boxes, content that reads top to bottom like a well-written page.

## Design Principles
1. Read like a document, not an app. Hierarchy comes from type and spacing; boxes only where something is genuinely separate.
2. Lead with the answer. The first screen says how the store did and what to fix first, in a sentence.
3. Let the store's own numbers carry the weight. Specific figures and page names, no decorative charts.
4. Calm severity. Problems are clear without shouting: color marks severity in small doses, never floods a surface.
5. One brand, two languages. English and Arabic are equal citizens; RTL must feel designed, not flipped.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA: body text 4.5:1, large text 3:1, visible keyboard focus, no information carried by color alone (severity always has a word), respects prefers-reduced-motion, readable when printed or saved as PDF.
