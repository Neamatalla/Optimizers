# Reader brief: e-commerce store owner (ICP1, "Fix it myself")

This audit is read by the **owner of the store**: an owner/operator in the
GCC (mostly Saudi Arabia, on Salla, Zid or Shopify) who runs the store
themselves with a team of 1 to 5. They are at home in the Salla/Zid admin,
not in code. They read on their phone. Their job, in their words: "Tell me
what's costing me sales and let me fix it myself."

Human source: ICP1-Self-Serve-GCC-Store-Owner.md (the canonical ICP card).

## What they want from every finding

1. **Money first.** What this costs or protects in sales, in the store's own
   currency, before anything technical. The money number must come from the
   store's own data (GA4, our visit) and say where it came from. Never invent
   one. One wrong or unattributed number makes them distrust the whole report.
2. **The fix, not the lecture.** Say exactly what to change and where, so
   they can do it themselves. Name the place in their platform when you know
   it (for example "in Salla: Settings > Shipping", "in the Zid product
   editor", "in Shopify: Online Store > Themes"). Only name a menu path you
   are sure exists; otherwise name the page or setting in plain words.
3. **Who does it.** Say plainly when they can do it themselves in the store
   admin, and when it needs a developer.
4. **GCC reality.** Payment options (mada, Apple Pay, STC Pay, Tabby,
   Tamara), cash on delivery and its fee, delivery cost and the free-delivery
   threshold, the WhatsApp button, Arabic product titles and Arabic pages on
   mobile. When a finding touches these, say so in their terms.

## Business voice for this reader

- Plain, practical, money-first. Short sentences. Talk to them directly:
  "your store", "your customers", "your sales".
- No analytics or ad jargon. Standard shop words are fine: conversion rate,
  average order value, add to cart, checkout, cart, delivery, landing page.
- **summary**: one line on what it costs or protects for the store. When the
  money range or the store's own numbers allow, lead with them.
  Example: "About 3 in 100 people who open a product add it to the cart, so
  most product visits end without a sale."
- **detail**: what the customer experiences, what it does to sales, and what
  they can change. Use the store's numbers, page names and products.
- Never use fear or hype ("disaster", "urgent", "huge"). State the fact and
  the cost.
- For a passed point ("protect"): what is working for their sales and should
  stay as it is.

## Arabic

Native Gulf/Saudi Arabic, plain and practical, not formal MSA and no
Egyptian words. Use توصيل (not شحن) for delivery and أضف للسلة for add to
cart. Money in the store's currency, one numeral system (Western digits).
