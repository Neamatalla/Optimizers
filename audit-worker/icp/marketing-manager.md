# Reader brief: marketing director / manager (ICP3, "Show my CEO and team what to fix first")

This audit is read by a **marketing director or marketing manager** (also an
e-commerce manager, CRO or growth lead) at a mid-size store or brand in KSA,
the UAE or Egypt. They work with the CEO above them and a team (developers,
designers, copywriters, media buyers or an agency) beside them. They are
non-technical to mid-technical. They use the audit to win budget and
priority from the CEO and to brief the team. Their job: "Present a credible,
data-driven audit so we can prioritize the fixes that boost sales."

Human source: ICP3-Marketing-Director-Manager.md (the canonical ICP card).

## What they want from every finding

1. **Priority and payoff.** How much it matters to sales (use the store's
   numbers and the money ranges code added) so they can rank it against
   everything else on the roadmap.
2. **Who on the team owns it.** Developer, designer/content, marketing or
   media buying, or the owner/CEO (pricing, delivery, offer). A finding
   should be ready to hand over as a brief.
3. **Evidence they can defend.** One wrong number in front of the CEO hurts
   their credibility. State where each number comes from (GA4, the store
   visit, the tag setup) and never invent one.
4. **The KPIs they own:** conversion by traffic source, mobile vs desktop
   conversion, ROAS / CAC / ROI, local payment methods (mada, Apple Pay, STC
   Pay, Tabby, Tamara), Arabic and mobile experience, privacy and compliance
   (PDPL, consent).

## ROAS and CAC

This audit doesn't see ad spend: never state a ROAS, CAC or spend value. When
a checklist point carries an "Ad effect when failing" line, say that
direction in the detail as an estimate (for example "this likely lowers CAC
once fixed", or "reported ROAS currently reads higher than real").

## Business voice for this reader

- Clear, professional and concise, like a briefing note. Short sentences.
- Standard marketing and e-commerce terms are fine: conversion rate, AOV,
  ROAS, CAC, channel, landing page, funnel, attribution. Explain analytics
  and tag internals in a few words.
- **summary**: one line a CEO would understand, naming the business effect
  (sales, conversion, spend that can't be judged, risk).
  Example: "Paid Social brings 44,272 visits a month but only 164 sales,
  the weakest channel in the store."
- **detail**: the business impact, which decision or report it distorts, and
  which team should act. Concrete, with the store's numbers.
- Speak about "the store", "the team" and "the business"; address the reader
  as "you" where natural.
- No hype, no selling.
- For a passed point ("protect"): what is working and should be kept as it
  is while the team works on other fixes.

## Arabic

Clear, professional Modern Standard Arabic suitable for sharing with a
mixed Arabic/English team and the CEO. On first use, keep the English KPI
name in brackets, for example معدل التحويل (Conversion Rate).
