## 1. The one-line truth

**A media buyer is paid for a number (ROAS / CAC) that depends on two things they don't control: the tracking that measures it and the website that converts it.** [Core + Inferred]

- **Juniors** feel this as a *technical* problem: "my events don't fire and I don't know why."
- **Seniors** feel it as a *strategic* problem: "I've squeezed the ad account. The next 30% of ROAS lives in the bottom of the funnel, and I don't own it."

Optimizers sells both of them the missing half: **trustworthy data + a site that converts the traffic they already paid for.**

## 4. Shared core: what every media buyer has in common

### 4.1 They live and die by ROAS [Core]
- ROAS is checked every morning. One bad week can cost a client, a bonus, or a job.
- Every decision (scale, cut, new creative) is filtered through "will this move ROAS / CAC?"
- Anything we say has to end in **ROAS up or CAC down**, or they stop listening.

### 4.2 The environment keeps getting harder [Research]
- **CAC is rising:** reported up about 40% in two years; MENA CPCs reported up 40–50% over five years on Google and Meta.
- **Signal loss:** iOS, Safari ITP, ad blockers and consent rules hide conversions. When the algorithm can't see conversions it optimizes on partial data, audiences shrink, and reported ROAS understates reality.
- **Platform ROAS is inflated:** 15–30% above real ROAS in multi-platform accounts, because each platform credits itself.
- **Buying skill is being automated:** with Meta's Andromeda-era delivery, sources argue the growth lever has moved from media buying to the creative pipeline, and that a buyer can no longer make up for weak creative through audience tricks. **[Inferred]** The buyer's remaining edges are creative, data quality, and post-click conversion. Optimizers owns two of those three.

### 4.3 They think they know CRO [Core]
- In practice they implement **best practices**: sticky add-to-cart, countdown timer, reviews widget, free-shipping bar, trust badges.
- That's copying, not CRO. Real CRO = **diagnose with data → find the specific leak on this store → fix → measure**.
- When the checklist is done and ROAS is still flat, they're out of ideas. That gap is our opening.

### 4.4 They don't own the two things that decide their number [Inferred]
- **Tracking:** GTM, theme code and apps belong to the store owner or a developer.
- **Website:** design, checkout, offer, shipping, payment options belong to the store owner.
- So they see problems but can't fix them, and still get blamed.

### 4.5 Universal fears

| Fear | Who feels it most |
|---|---|
| ROAS drops and they lose the account | Everyone [Core] |
| CAC climbs past what the margin can carry | Strategist [Core + Research] |
| They scale a campaign that only looks good because tracking double-counts | Technician and Operator [Research] |
| They kill a campaign that was working because tracking undercounts | Everyone [Research] |
| Someone else shows the client a problem they missed | Operator [Inferred] |
| Being seen as "just a button pusher" | Technician and Strategist, for different reasons [Inferred] |
| Being replaced by automation (Advantage+, PMax, AI buyers) | Technician [Inferred from Research] |

## 5. The ROAS equation: why CRO is their lever

This is the single most useful framing for every conversation. **Speak their language: ROAS and CAC, not "UX".**

```
ROAS          = Revenue / Ad spend
              = (Clicks × Conversion rate × AOV) / (Clicks × CPC)
              = (Conversion rate × AOV) / CPC

CPA (or CAC)  = CPC / Conversion rate
```

**Translation:** ROAS has three levers. The media buyer controls only **CPC** (partly). **Conversion rate and AOV live on the website.** [Inferred, standard math]

### Worked example (SAR, illustrative)

| Scenario | CPC | Conv. rate | AOV | ROAS | CPA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Today | 2.00 | 1.5% | 250 | **1.88** | 133 |
| Fix one funnel leak (CR → 2.0%) | 2.00 | 2.0% | 250 | **2.50** | **100 (−25%)** |
| + Bundle / threshold (AOV +15%) | 2.00 | 2.0% | 288 | **2.88** | 100 |
| + Better landing page lifts Meta / Google auction quality (CPC −10%) | 1.80 | 2.0% | 288 | **3.20** | 90 |

- Same budget, same ads: ROAS **1.88 → 3.20**. No media buying change could do that alone.
- Raising conversion from 3% to 6% at the same spend halves cost per result. [Research]
- A 15% AOV lift = 15% ROAS lift at the same spend, if conversion holds. [Research]

### Why the landing page also changes CPC [Research]
- **Meta:** the auction ranks by bid × estimated action rate (+/× ad quality). Meta looks at the post-click experience; a click followed by a bounce is a negative signal. A page that converts better raises the estimated action rate, which works like a higher bid without paying more.
- **Google:** landing page experience is a major Quality Score component. Sources cite 30–50% lower CPC at top scores; "above average" landing page experience plus ad relevance is linked to about 36% lower CPCs.

### Why tracking changes ROAS without any real change [Inferred from Research]
- **Undercount example:** purchase event fires for only 70% of orders. Meta reports ROAS 1.75 when real ROAS is 2.5. The buyer cuts a winning campaign, and the algorithm learns from a 70% sample.
- **Overcount example:** purchase fires twice (pixel + CAPI without a shared event_id, or a refreshed thank-you page). Meta reports ROAS 3.0 when real ROAS is 1.5. The buyer scales into a loss.

**So the pitch to every tier:** "Before you touch a bid, make sure the number is real (tracking) and the traffic has a place to convert (site)."

## 6. CRO overlap: how far each tier wants to go

### 6.1 The CRO depth ladder [Inferred]

| Level | What it is | Typical owner |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | "CRO is the client's problem" | Very junior or pure-execution buyers |
| 1 | **Best-practice checklist** (sticky ATC, timers, badges, reviews app) | Technician, many Operators [Core] |
| 2 | **Message match:** landing page matches the ad's promise, offer and language | Operator |
| 3 | **Funnel diagnosis:** find which step leaks (view_item → add_to_cart → checkout → purchase) and why, per device / page / campaign | Strategist |
| 4 | **Hypothesis + testing:** prioritized backlog, A/B tests, landing pages per campaign / audience | Strategist with resources |
| 5 | **Full-funnel economics:** AOV, offer design, payment mix, COD confirmation, retention / LTV, margin-aware ROAS | Head of growth |

### 6.2 How far each tier will go

| | Technician | Operator | Strategist |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sits at | Level 1 | Level 1–2 | Level 2–4 |
| Wants to reach | Level 2, plus tracking literacy | Level 3, with proof they can show the client | Level 4–5, done by a partner |
| Wants to *do* CRO themselves? | No. Wants to understand it and pass it on | Partly: quick wins they can push | No. Wants to direct it and own the outcome |
| Time they'll give it | Minutes (a checklist) | An hour (a report to forward) | A project (a roadmap, a sprint) |
| Where they stop | The moment it needs dev, design or code | When the client won't act | When it doesn't map to ROAS / CAC / margin |
| What "done" means to them | Events fire, numbers match | ROAS recovered, client stays | ROAS / CAC moved, proven with data |

**Key rule [Core]:** they want CRO **only as far as it moves ROAS and CAC**. Every CRO point we show must be framed as a ROAS or CAC effect, never as "better UX" for its own sake.

## 7. Persona A: The Technician (junior / starting media buyer)

### 7.1 Persona card [Inferred, composite]

> **Youssef, 24. Junior media buyer at a Cairo agency serving Saudi Salla and Shopify stores.**
> 1.5 years in. Learned from YouTube and a paid course. Runs day-to-day Meta, TikTok and Snap campaigns for 6 client stores under a senior buyer. Has Events Manager, Pixel Helper and GTM preview open most of the day. Last week a client's purchases dropped to zero in Meta while Salla showed 40 orders. He spent two days on it and still doesn't know why. His senior blamed "iOS".

### 7.2 Role and day-to-day [Research: job postings]
- Launches and optimizes campaigns, runs daily performance checks, reports results.
- Makes small changes (bids, creative swaps) under senior guidance.
- Sets up creatives and tests.
- Often the one asked to "check the pixel".

### 7.3 Goals
1. **Make the tracking work and understand why it broke.** [Core]
2. Look competent to their senior and the client.
3. Get promoted: become the person who "knows tracking". At senior level, tracking and server-side setup is a named skill. [Research]
4. Hit the CPA / ROAS targets they're given.

### 7.4 Pain points (ranked) [Core + Research + Inferred]
1. **Events don't fire or fire wrong, and they can't find the cause.** Purchase missing, ATC firing when nothing was added, duplicates, wrong value or currency.
2. **Numbers don't match.** Meta vs GA4 vs store orders vs TikTok / Snap: four different numbers and no way to say which is right.
3. **No access.** Can't edit the theme, GTM is owned by a developer, checkout can't be customized (Shopify non-Plus, Salla / Zid limits).
4. **Debugging tools lie or don't work.** On Shopify custom pixels (sandboxed), GTM Preview doesn't work, so they can't see checkout events. [Research]
5. **"Connected" doesn't mean working.** Shopify can show the Meta pixel as connected while CAPI is silently broken. [Research]
6. **Too many overlapping installs.** Platform app + GTM + hard-coded snippet = duplicates or conflicts. [Research]
7. **Can't explain it to the client** without sounding like they're making excuses.
8. **Afraid to break things** in GTM or the theme.

### 7.5 What they believe vs reality

| They believe | Reality |
|---|---|
| "Pixel Helper shows green, so it works" | Green = it fired. Not that the value, currency, event_id or user data are right |
| "CAPI is on, so tracking is solved" | CAPI without a shared event_id double-counts; low match quality means Meta can't use the events well [Research] |
| "The drop is iOS" | Often it's a broken event, a gateway redirect, or a site change [Research + Inferred] |
| "CRO = install the right apps" | Apps are best practices; they don't find the leak [Core] |

### 7.6 Their words (search terms and phrases) [Inferred]
- "purchase event not firing shopify / salla / zid"
- "meta purchases not matching orders"
- "duplicate purchase events meta"
- "event match quality low how to fix"
- "add to cart firing but no purchase"
- "GTM preview not working shopify checkout"
- "snap pixel not tracking purchase" / "tiktok events not showing"
- Arabic (Egyptian): "البيكسل مش بيسجل الشراء", "الـ Purchase مش بيظهر في Events Manager", "الأوردرات في المتجر أكتر من اللي في ميتا"
- Arabic (Gulf): "البكسل ما يسجل المشتريات", "سناب بكسل ما يقرأ الشراء"

### 7.7 Where they learn [Inferred: validate]
- YouTube (Arabic media-buying channels), paid courses, Facebook groups and Telegram groups for Arab media buyers
- Reddit (r/FacebookAds, r/PPC), Meta / TikTok / Snap help centers, Shopify Community forums
- Their senior buyer

### 7.8 Triggers
- Purchase count in Meta suddenly drops while store orders don't
- Client asks "why does Meta say 20 and Salla says 45?"
- Events Manager warning: duplicates, low match quality, missing parameters
- New store onboarding: "set up the pixel"
- Theme change or new app installed, and tracking breaks

### 7.9 What wins them
- **Explanations, not jargon:** "Here's why it doesn't fire, here's how to prove it, here's the fix."
- **Checklists and step-by-step diagnostics** they can run themselves.
- **A forwardable report** that proves the problem with evidence, so they can escalate to the developer or client.
- **Credit:** they look like the expert.

### 7.10 Value to Optimizers
- **Not the buyer:** little budget.
- **The champion and the door:** they find the problem, forward our report, and pull us into the account. They become Operators and Strategists later.
- **Volume:** many of them, active in communities, good for content reach.

## 8. Event failure library (the content Persona A craves)

Every row is a real, common cause. Use these as content posts, a lead-magnet checklist, and audit framing.

| # | Symptom | Likely cause | How to confirm | Fix | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Purchase missing in Meta, orders exist | Wrong pixel ID, or CAPI silently disconnected / data sharing not on "Maximum" | Test Events while placing a test order; check the Facebook & Instagram app's data sharing setting | Correct the ID; reconnect CAPI; set data sharing to Maximum | [Research] |
| 2 | Purchases counted twice | Pixel + CAPI without matching `event_id` + event name | Events Manager dedup / overlap warnings; compare to orders | Generate one event ID at the action, send it from both pixel and server | [Research: Meta docs] |
| 3 | Purchases counted twice (GA4) | Thank-you page refresh fires again; GTM + hard-coded gtag both send purchase; old tag + new pixel both live | Two purchases with the same order seconds apart; Network tab | One owner of the purchase event; unique `transaction_id` per order | [Research] |
| 4 | Revenue missing or wrong | `value` sent as text ("SAR 599"), missing `currency`, value before discount / tax, last item price only, no `items` array | Check the GA4 / Meta request payload in the Network tab | Send final charged amount as a number + ISO currency + items | [Research] |
| 5 | Checkout and purchase events missing on Shopify | Checkout extensibility: tracking runs in a sandboxed custom pixel; GTM Preview doesn't work there; some Meta browser events from checkout only arrive via CAPI | console.log in the custom pixel, GA4 Realtime, Network tab | Use the platform's native integration or a proper server-side setup; validate at network level | [Research] |
| 6 | Add_to_cart fires but nothing was added | Trigger on button click, not on a confirmed add (AJAX cart) | Click ATC, then check cart contents | Fire on the cart API success / cart update, not the click | [Audits: tharaa] |
| 7 | Add_to_cart never fires | AJAX / drawer cart doesn't reload the page; click trigger on a CSS class that changed; Arabic / RTL URLs not matched | GTM preview on the product page; dataLayer contents | Use the platform's dataLayer events or a cart-API listener | [Inferred] |
| 8 | Purchases lost after online payment | Payment gateway (Tabby, Tamara, Tap, PayTabs…) redirect; shopper closes tab before the thank-you page | Orders paid by gateway vs purchase events; GA4 sessions from the gateway domain | Server-side purchase event; gateway domains in GA4's unwanted referrals | [Inferred + Audits: GA4-19] |
| 9 | Gateways show as traffic sources in GA4 | Return from the gateway starts a new session with the gateway as source | GA4 source/medium report | Add gateway domains to the referral exclusion list | [Audits] |
| 10 | GA4 "conversions" impossible (rates over 100%) | Non-purchase events (view_item, add_to_cart, video…) marked as key events | Key event list vs real purchases | Only real business outcomes as key events; report sales from transactions | [Audits: regal-honey had 21 key events] |
| 11 | Low Event Match Quality | Missing customer info (email, phone, external_id, fbc / fbp), inconsistent hashing | Events Manager EMQ score per event | Send hashed email + phone + click IDs with server events | [Research] |
| 12 | Events missing for a share of users | Ad blockers, Safari ITP, tab closed before pixel fires | Server vs browser event counts | Add server-side events (CAPI / Events API) with dedup | [Research] |
| 13 | Conversions drop after a consent banner | Consent default "denied"; CMP blocks tags; Consent Mode v2 parameters not all mapped | Tag status by consent state | Map all four v2 parameters; modeled conversions need volume thresholds | [Research] |
| 14 | Duplicate tracking hits from the platform | Platform's own collector or native pixel + GTM both sending | Network tab: two hits per event | Choose one source of truth per platform | [Audits: Zid + extra trackers on regal-honey] |
| 15 | COD / WhatsApp orders invisible to ads | Order placed off-site or confirmed later; no pixel fires | Orders by channel vs purchase events | Send confirmed orders via CAPI; for click-to-WhatsApp, store `ctwa_clid` and send the purchase back | [Research] |
| 16 | Big gap between link clicks and landing page views | Slow landing page; pixel loads late | LPV ÷ link clicks (70–90% is typical; a >20% gap points to the site) | Speed up the page; load the pixel early | [Research] |
| 17 | Snap / TikTok purchases don't match | No Conversions / Events API, no `event_id` dedup, missing `transaction_id` | Snap Pixel Helper / TikTok Test Events on a test order | Pair pixel with server API and shared IDs | [Research] |
| 18 | Changes "don't work" | GTM workspace edited but not published; wrong container on site | GTM versions; container ID in page source | Publish; remove stray containers | [Inferred] |
| 19 | Wrong currency in reports | Store in SAR, events sent in USD (or no currency) | Event payload | Send the store's real currency | [Inferred] |

**Diagnosis tools to name in content:** Meta Events Manager Test Events, Meta Pixel Helper, Snap Pixel Helper, TikTok Test Events, GA4 DebugView and Realtime, browser Network tab, GTM Preview (not on Shopify checkout). [Research]

## 9. Persona B: The Strategist (performance manager / head of paid / growth lead)

### 9.1 Persona card [Inferred, composite]

> **Rana, 32. Head of performance at a Riyadh fashion brand doing about SAR 1.5M/month online, or the paid lead at a regional agency.**
> 8 years in. Manages a team of 3 buyers and about SAR 400k/month across Meta, Snap, TikTok and Google. Reports to the founder and CFO on blended ROAS and CAC. Ads are "optimized to death": creative testing is running, structure is clean. Last quarter, scaling spend 30% dropped blended ROAS from 3.1 to 2.4. She suspects the site (mobile Arabic product pages, shipping cost revealed late, COD refusals) but the dev team is busy, and she needs numbers to win the argument.

### 9.2 Role [Research: job postings + Inferred]
- Owns paid media strategy, budget allocation and reporting across platforms.
- Mentors buyers; works with brand, creative, product and dev teams.
- Owns attribution and tracking decisions at a high level.
- Accountable for blended outcomes, not one ad set.

### 9.3 KPIs they think in [Research]
- **MER / blended ROAS** = total revenue ÷ total marketing spend. The "ROAS you can scale against".
- **CAC and new-customer CAC (nCAC)**; new vs returning MER tracked separately.
- **Break-even ROAS** = 1 ÷ contribution margin (50% margin → 2.0x).
- **Contribution margin per order minus fully loaded CAC.**
- **LTV:CAC** (2:1–4:1 cited as healthy) and **CAC payback** (median DTC about 3.4 months; over 12 months = cash-constrained).
- **Incrementality:** holdouts, brand-search-off tests.

### 9.4 Goals
1. **Raise blended ROAS / lower CAC at higher spend.** [Core]
2. **Find levers outside the ad account**, mainly the bottom of the funnel. [Core]
3. Prove impact to the founder / CFO with money, not opinions.
4. Get the dev and brand teams to act on the right things first.
5. Make the algorithm stronger by feeding it clean, complete conversion data.

### 9.5 Pain points (ranked) [Core + Research + Inferred]
1. **Scaling makes returns shrink.** More spend → colder traffic → the site's weaknesses show → ROAS falls.
2. **The ad account is no longer the edge.** Creative and algorithm do most of the work. Their value has to come from somewhere else. [Research + Inferred]
3. **They know the site leaks but can't prove where or how much.** "Mobile converts worse" isn't a business case; "the product page loses SAR 180k/month on mobile" is.
4. **No dev or CRO capacity.** Dev is busy. Testing needs traffic and tools they don't have.
5. **Measurement they can't fully trust:** platforms over-credit, consent and iOS under-count, GA4 setups are polluted. Decisions get made on bad numbers. [Research]
6. **COD economics hide the real ROAS** (refusals, fake orders, returns). [Research + Inferred]
7. **Political friction:** brand team wants pretty, founder wants cheap, dev wants fewer tickets.
8. **Bottom-funnel channels get over-credited** (brand search, retargeting); upper-funnel gets starved. One source says incrementality tests often find 60–80% of retargeting revenue would have happened anyway. [Research]

### 9.6 What they believe vs reality

| They believe | Reality |
|---|---|
| "We've done CRO: the theme is clean, we have reviews and urgency" | That's level 1. The leak is usually store-specific: a step, a device, a page, a payment option [Core] |
| "ROAS target 3x is good" | Only relative to margin. 3x on a 30% margin is about breakeven [Research] |
| "Retargeting is our best channel" | Often largely non-incremental [Research] |
| "We can't do CRO without A/B testing traffic" | Diagnosis + fixing clear leaks doesn't need a test; tests are for uncertain bets [Inferred] |

### 9.7 What they want from CRO (and how far) [Core + Inferred]
- **Usability and friction findings on the pages their traffic actually lands on**, per device.
- **A prioritized list:** what to fix first, its estimated money effect, who owns it, how to know it's done.
- **Landing-page / campaign fit:** does each campaign land somewhere that converts?
- **Bottom-of-funnel levers:** checkout, payment mix, shipping clarity, AOV, COD confirmation.
- **They stop at:** anything that can't be tied to ROAS, CAC or margin.

### 9.8 Their words [Inferred]
- "We're scaling but efficiency is dropping"
- "Our MER is down, blended CAC is up"
- "Is the drop the site or the ads?"
- "Where are we losing people after the click?"
- "What should we fix first?"
- "Our CVR on mobile is half of desktop"
- "We need landing pages per campaign"
- Arabic: "الـ ROAS بيقع لما نزود الميزانية", "إيه أول حاجة نصلحها في الموقع؟", "الموبايل بيحوّل أقل بكتير"

### 9.9 Triggers
- Blended ROAS falls after a budget increase
- Big season coming (Ramadan, White Friday, National Day), where every point of CVR is worth more
- Founder / CFO pushback on CAC or payback
- Site redesign or platform migration (Salla → Shopify, etc.)
- New funding / growth targets that require more spend at the same efficiency

### 9.10 Value to Optimizers
- **The buyer:** budget authority or strong influence.
- **Best fit for paid engagements:** CRO roadmap, sprints, landing pages, tracking rebuild.
- **Repeat value:** monthly CRO retainer tied to ROAS / CAC goals.

Each lever is framed the way they think: **which ROAS term it moves.**

| Lever | Moves | Evidence / note | Source |
|---|---|---|---|

### The COD reality check (use in sales) [Inferred from Research]
```
Real ROAS = Reported ROAS × (1 − refusal rate) × (1 − return rate)

Reported 2.5 × (1 − 0.20) = 2.0 real
If break-even ROAS is 2.5 (40% margin) → the "profitable" campaign loses money.
```

## 13. "Ads or site?" diagnosis flow

Give this to every media buyer. It's the most useful 60 seconds we can offer, and it ends at us. [Research + Inferred]

```
ROAS dropped
│
├─ 1. Is the number real?  (TRACKING)
│     Compare platform purchases vs real orders for the same days.
│     Gap > ~20%, duplicates, or sudden zeroes → tracking problem. Fix first.
│
├─ 2. Did the site change?  (SITE)
│     Compare conversion rate and funnel steps (view_item → ATC → checkout → purchase)
│     before vs after, by device.
│     Site CVR fell while traffic quality held → the store is the leak.
│     Also check the link-click → landing-page-view rate (speed).
│
└─ 3. Did the ads change?  (MEDIA)
      CPM, CTR, CPC, frequency, creative fatigue, auction changes.
      CPM / CPA up while site CVR held → ads or platform.
```
